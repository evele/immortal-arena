# Item Stat Aggregate

This document summarizes normal market item stats from `docs/items-and-equipment.md`.

Special non-market items are excluded. Undead-only item lines are excluded from MVP race totals.

These are raw catalog sums, not a legal equipped build. A warrior can equip one Armour, one Weapon, and one Enchanted Item, but this table intentionally sums every available item in each bucket to compare item access by race.

## Notes

- Armour is universal for MVP races, so it does not differentiate race access.
- Hammer is universal for MVP races and heavily contributes Speed in the raw item catalog.
- Axe access is shared by Dwarf, Goblin, and Orc.
- Elf has extra access to Ranged and Staff.
- Human has extra access to Staff and Sword.
- Goblin, Dwarf, and Orc each have fewer total item lines than Elf and Human in the normal market catalog.
