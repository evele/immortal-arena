Undead are not part of the current MVP race list. MVP races remain Human, Orc, Elf, Dwarf, and Goblin.

## Later Design Notes

- Undead have very high Defense and low Speed compared with most other races.
- If added later, Undead need a place in the racial hatred cycle or a separate matchup rule.
- The source notes imply a long unlock gap between Skeleton Lord at level 16 and Spectre at level 35.
- Do not include Undead in MVP 0 implementation unless the MVP race list is explicitly changed.
