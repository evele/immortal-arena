Here's your legendary Iceman again - with an updated guide to help you through this game and yet, let you have all the enjoyment of experimenting with the various stats and races!

For level 1; starting out - Lord Level 1 - When starting, do NOT buy ANYTHING or a warrior. Just wait a day or so. Then, your money will accrue to the point where you can buy a warrior AND a weapon and/or armor for said warrior. This will put you a leg up on the other level 1s out there, possibly even some level 2s.

And don't FORGET to EQUIP the armor and weapons! From that point on, you have four different lord stats to improve as you level up: Money, Number of Attacks, Number of Training Matches, and Number of Warriors.

Money is useful for buying new warriors, armor, weapons and magic items. As you reach higher levels, the more expensive the warriors or accoutrements will be!

Number of Attacks increases by 1 each time you select this option - thus giving you one extra chance to wreak havoc and/or gain exp!

Number of Training Matches will increase by the number of warriors you currently have each time you select this option - so it makes sense to select this when you have already built a stable of frothing-at-the-mouth berserkers.

Number of Warriors will increase your stable of dwarves, elves, etc. by one each time you select this option - thus allowing you to create an army of sorts to win in the Arena.

As you level up, you can diversify your Lord points and spread them out, or you can pump one up if you wish!

As for your warriors: When you start out, you'll want to give them extra HP - so they can soak up some hits and be able to finish off your enemy. 200+ is a nice start; later on you can decide whether you want to continue that or put the stats elsewhere.

Race comes into play here for two reasons: Specific stats are the specialty of certain races. Elves are reknown for their accuracy, while Goblins are unmatched in speed. However, their damage and defense leave something to be desired. Dwarves and Orcs, on the other hand, have really bad speed and agility. But then again, Dwarves are very hardy, so their defense is top notch, while the Orcs have a lethal striking ability. While those stats make some players drool, the Dwarves' speed is lethargic, and the Orc's defense is abysmal. So I would make a point of beefing up those stats - or pump up the ones that they're feared for! The other reason the racial card comes into play - certain races hate another race.
But really, your warriors are your own preference - you can build them however way you like.

Explanation of the warrior stats as follows:
HP - Self explanatory - the more HP a warrior has, the amount of damage it takes to kill him.
Damage - Again, self explanatory. The more damage a warrior has, the harder they hit.
Defense - Another self explanatory one. The higher the defense, the more hits that will be absorbed. Accuracy - this one's a bit trickier. The higher this stat is, the more SKILLED hits you get - which results in a bit more damage, etc.
Agility - The higher the agility, the more often your warrior will AVOID getting hit. Kind of works in tandem with defense.
Speed - The higher the speed, the FASTER your warriors are and the more often they will attack.
