# Questions For Tomorrow

These are pending design questions to continue later. Do not ask all at once; use them as a backlog.

## Combat Formula

- Should the 100-action draw limit scale with the number of warriors in battle?
- What happens to rewards on a draw?
- How strong is the racial hatred damage bonus?

## Guild Vs Guild Resolution

- Can one strong warrior defeat multiple enemy warriors sequentially?
- Can multiple warriors target the same enemy?
- Does the player configure formation/priority before battle?
- Are wounded defending warriors included automatically at their current HP?

## Wounds And Recovery

- After battle, do warriors keep their remaining HP?
- How quickly do wounded warriors recover naturally?
- Can the player pay gold to heal instantly?
- Is there an Apothecary equivalent?
- Can a warrior attack while wounded?
- Should there be a minimum HP requirement to attack?

## Rewards

- How much lord experience does a win grant?
- How much warrior experience does a win grant?
- Do defeated warriors gain experience?
- Do all participating warriors gain experience or only surviving warriors?
- How is gold awarded?
- Should rewards scale by opponent strength like the original?
- How do we prevent farming weak/inactive accounts?

## Progression

- How many points does a warrior gain per level?
- How many points does a lord gain per level?
- What is the XP curve for warriors?
- What is the XP curve for lords?
- What is the max warrior level?
- Is level 100 the Immortal threshold?
- What does becoming Immortal actually do?

## MVP Boundaries

- Does the MVP need equipment?
- Does the MVP need training?
- Does the MVP need salary and daily reset from day one?
- Does the MVP need seasons from day one?
- Does the MVP need leaderboard from day one?
