# Blood Arena Beginner's Guide

The following is a basic guide to playing Blood Arena. It is recommended that you read this over before beginning to play.

## The Object Of Blood Arena

In Blood Arena there is no "beating the game" per se. You represent a guild, competing with everyone else playing to be the richest, most powerful guild. You must buy warriors, equip them, train them, and lead them into battle.

## Guild Profile

This is the main screen of the game; you are sent here every time you log in. Guild Profile gives you access to all your statistics and information about yourself, like battles won/lost, your salary, etc.

## First Things First: Change Your Password

Before anything, we highly recommend that you change your password to something other than the random password you are provided with when you sign up. To do so click the account settings link.

## Money In BloodArena

The currency in Blood Arena is the gold crown. It is used for purchasing warriors and items.

## Warriors

In order to defend yourself and attack others you need to purchase warriors. This is the main idea behind Blood Arena. To get to your main warriors screen, click warriors in the sidebar. From here you can manage your warriors.

Your warriors can be one of several races; orc, human, elf, dwarf and goblin are the main ones (but there may be more exotic ones available at higher levels!). Each race has some animosity to one other race and this can effect battles. If a warrior attacks another warrior of a hated race, they will attack much more viciously.

Orcs hate humans, humans hate goblins, goblins hate elves, elves hate dwarfs and dwarfs hate orcs.

For donators there is also a choice of an additional race: Undead. The undead races get a very small racial bonus against all other races except for Elves. Elves have a natural hatred of such evil beings and will battle with extreme vigour against undead warriors.

To purchase a warrior you must visit the Market by clicking on the appropriate link. From the buy section you will be able to examine different warriors and choose one to purchase. At Level 1 you will not have a large selection from which to pick, however as you move up through the game more and more options become available.

You now have a Level 1 warrior and you can begin attacking other Guilds.

## Items

Now that you have purchased a warrior, you can improve his attributes by equipping items to him.

Items are divided into classes. Each warrior can only have one item from each item class equipped. Each item class is further divided into subclasses. If one item class has three subclasses you will only be able to equip one item from all three of those subclasses at one time. Subclasses exist to limit which items certain warriors can use. When you view your warrior details you will see which item subclasses they have access to. You will not be able to equip anything out of these subclasses.

Items can be purchased by clicking the Armoury link. Be sure that the subclass of the item you are purchasing was listed as one that your warrior has access to. Also remember that each warrior can only have one item from each class equipped at one time. In addition to this, all items have minimum Level that your warrior must meet. New warriors are only Level 1.

Now that you've purchased an item for your warrior, you must equip it to them. To do so, click the Storeroom link. You will not be able to equip an item to a warrior if he doesn't have access to the item subclass, if he already has an item equipped in that class, or if he doesn't have a high enough Level.

## Training

To train up your warrior you can visit the Training Arena. By training your warriors can improve without actually having to fight real battles versus other Guilds.

## Battling

This is the main point of Blood Arena; to fight and see who wins. To start, click the Blood Arena link. From there, you can either type in a guild name to attack or search for someone.

If you win your battle, your guild will get experience, and your remaining warriors will get also get experience. The amount of experience your guild and warriors receive is equal. In addition to this, you receive a prize of gold, equal to half the experience you earned.

When you get to a certain number of experience (see your Guild Profile page for the exact number) you will go up a Level. Moving up Levels earns you Guild Points. These points can be applied towords enhancing your guild further (such as increasing number of warriors, number of attacks you can make per day etc) daily salary, etc.

The amount of and that you receive depends on the you are fighting. Defeating a stronger will yield more than a weak one.

Note that normally 1 Guild Point allows you to increase 1 guild attribute. However after your guild has reached 25 attacks, it will require 2 Guild Points per additional attack from that point on.

## The Tournament

Once a week there is a tournament which you can enter for a fee. The cost to enter the tournament depends on your guild level - the higher level you are the more you have to pay. Prizes in the tournament either be gold or on occasion, a unique item that can be used by your warriors.

## Reviving

When you battle, it is inevitable that some of your warriors will take damage. To heal a warrior up to full HP simply visit the Apothecary link.

If you can't or won't pay to restore HP to your warriors you can also wait until they are automatically revived, which happens every 15 minutes.

## Server Resets

Every day at 12 noon and 12 midnight (UK Time) there is a server reset, at which time you will be paid your salary and alloted your daily battles and training sessions. Daily battles and training sessions will accumulate up to a maximum of four times your daily limit.

## Rounds

Every few months the game resets entirely and all players start from scratch. Check out the Rounds section for more information.

## Special Attribute

At the start of a new round, a warrior attribute (damage, defence, speed, agility etc) is selected at random as a "Special Attribute". All warriors will receive a percentage bonus to this attribute for the entire round.

## Fame

Fame points are awarded to guilds who finish in the top 10 position of certain categories. These categories are marked on the round summary page. Fame points are slightly different from other scores because they are not reset at the end of each round and are accumulated over time. Fame points give an indication of the long-term guild ranking. Donating players will also be able to use fame points to gain additional features in the game.
