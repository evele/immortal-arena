# Immortal Arena Vision

Immortal Arena is a BloodArena-inspired browser game adapted for blockchain ownership.

The player represents a lord who manages a roster of warriors. The lord buys warriors, equips them, trains them, sends them into asynchronous battles, earns rewards, and competes in seasonal rankings.

The project should preserve much of the early-2000s webgame structure: text-first interface, limited daily actions, automated combat, slow strategic progression, and competitive guild/lord management.

At the same time, the experience should be redesigned where blockchain adds real value: persistent ownership, tradable warriors, durable account identity, and seasonal continuity beyond normal resets.

## Current Direction

- The game should remain quite close to BloodArena in core structure.
- The experience can be redesigned where blockchain ownership makes the game better.
- The player identity is currently best described as a lord.
- Warriors should start with clear ownership and stable IDs, with NFTs or equivalent on-chain ownership as a later integration goal.
- The lord starts with gold and chooses the first warrior from the market.
- Equipment may also become NFTs, but this is undecided.
- A game token should be avoided initially.
- In-game money does not need to be on-chain at the start.
- Combat should be automatic and asynchronous.
- The first UI can be text-based, similar to classic browser games.
- Seasons are likely desirable, but what persists across seasons is still undecided.

## Design Pillars

- Strategic roster management over direct combat control.
- Automated PvP battles resolved from stats, equipment, race, and progression.
- Daily action limits that create pacing and long-term planning.
- Blockchain ownership without forcing every game action on-chain.
- Seasonal competition with some form of persistent legacy.
- Low-cost chain support suitable for frequent webgame interactions.
